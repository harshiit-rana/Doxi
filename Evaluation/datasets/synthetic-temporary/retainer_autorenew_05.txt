MARKETING RETAINER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of 01.10.2026 and is entered into between Brightpath Education Private Limited ("Client") and Studio Kite, a partnership firm ("Agency").

1. SERVICES
The Agency shall provide monthly social media management and performance marketing services as described in Annexure A.

2. FEES
2.1 The Client shall pay the Agency a monthly retainer of INR 1.5 lakh plus applicable GST, payable in advance on the 1st day of every month.
2.2 Invoices not paid within 30 days shall attract a late fee of 2% per month.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL
3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of twelve (12) months, ending on 30.09.2027.
3.2 Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiry of the then-current term.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall treat the other party's business information as confidential.
