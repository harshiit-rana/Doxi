ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACT

This Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is signed on 28-08-2026 between CoolAir Services, Indore (the "Service Provider") and Rana Digital Studio, New Delhi (the "Customer").

The Service Provider will service the office air conditioners every quarter during the contract period of 1 September 2026 to 31 August 2027.

AMC charges: Rs. 18,000 per annum, payable in advance on or before 01/09/2026.

The contract will be renewed automatically for another year at the then prevailing rates unless the Customer informs otherwise at least 30 days before expiry.
