Collaboration Agreement

This agreement dated 15.09.2026 is between Zestly Foods Pvt. Ltd. (the Brand) and Riya Sen (the Creator).

Deliverables: The Creator shall publish 3 Instagram reels about the Brand's new snack range on or before 31.10.2026.

Fee: The Brand will pay the Creator Rs. 45,000 plus GST within 30 days of the last reel going live.

Exclusivity: The Creator will not promote competing snack brands until 31.12.2026.
